The measured vectorised benchmark crosses over near one million generated orders: pandas took 43.374 s and local Spark 40.919 s, as medians of three measured runs after a warm-up. At two million, the 4 GiB memory guard stopped pandas while Spark completed in 63.169 s with disk spill; five million was excluded by the projected disk budget, not attempted. This is one laptop session on a defined cleaning/aggregation subset, not the production pipeline's stateful validation engine, so it supports a bounded scale decision rather than a general Spark speed claim. Full timing, memory, spill and equality limits are in the [benchmark report](../benchmarks.md).
